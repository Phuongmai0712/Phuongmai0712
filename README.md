## 📌 Featured Research

### Bitcoin & Gold under Economic Policy Uncertainty and Geopolitical Risk

Quantitative financial research examining whether **Bitcoin or gold provides defensive portfolio value during periods of economic and geopolitical uncertainty**.

**Methods & tools:** Python · pandas · NumPy · statsmodels · GARCH / EGARCH / GJR-GARCH · Dynamic Conditional Correlation (DCC) · econometric testing · bootstrap inference · portfolio optimisation

**Key finding:** Bitcoin remained positively correlated with equities throughout the sample, while gold showed substantially lower equity correlation and provided stronger portfolio risk reduction.
